Lead email kit: instant reply + video follow-up

Two emails for new Facebook Lead Ads leads, sent from a Google Workspace address. Table layout and inline styles, so Gmail and Outlook show the same thing. Fictional company, sample data.

Facebook Lead Ads→Zapier or Make→Email 1 from Gmail, instantly→Lead logged in Google Sheet→Booking marks the row→After the delay: Email 2 only to leads not booked

Email 1: instant auto-reply with a 4-step roadmap

Email 2: follow-up with an animated video thumbnail

What's inside

  • Gmail and Outlook. Tables, inline styles, a bulletproof button (VML for Outlook desktop). Each email stays well under 100 KB, so Gmail doesn't clip it.
  • Animated thumbnail. thumb.gif, 8 frames, about 13 KB. The play button is on frame one, because older Outlook shows only the first frame.
  • Video page on your domain. intro.html: player area for an unlisted Vimeo or Bunny Stream video (no YouTube branding or suggested videos) with the booking calendar right below.
  • Versions per industry. One template, a few fields: {{industry_line}}, {{industry_name}}, plus {{first_name}} from the lead form. A new version is a new row of text, not a new email.
  • Who gets Email 2. Every lead goes into a Google Sheet. The booking tool (Calendly or a Google Calendar booking page) marks the row. After the delay, the scenario sends Email 2 only to rows still unmarked.
