Role Play Coach

One agent, many scenarios. The learner talks to an AI customer, a second pass scores the conversation against the scenario rubric and quotes the exact lines behind each score, then the learner gets a PDF coaching guide. The score goes to the LMS as an xAPI statement. Each scenario is a config, so trainers change scenarios without touching code.

How it plugs into the LMS

        
Launch. With LTI 1.3 the learner opens the role play from a course page, and the agent gets the learner ID and course from the launch.

        
Score back. The overall score goes to the gradebook through LTI Assignment and Grade Services, or as the xAPI statement on this page to your LRS. The rubric scores travel with it, so trainers can see which skill is weak.

        
Checks before anything is saved. Every rubric item must be scored once, scores must be 1 to 5, and every quote must really appear in the learner's lines. If the scoring reply fails these checks, the session goes to a human instead of the gradebook.
